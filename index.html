Ziantech Solutions was established in December 2011 and began operating in the local ICT market in January 2014. Although a relatively new company, our team possesses a combined experience of more than 20 years in the industry. Each member of our team has previously held positions in various segments and business units within the local ICT scene, including Principals, Software Development Houses, and multi-million RM turnover local System Integrator companies. Our "HANDS-ON" approach to our work enables us to adapt to the evolving nature of the business and anticipate future trends.
