Main

Ziantech Solutions was established in December 2011 and began operating in the local ICT market in January 2014. Although a relatively new company, our team possesses a combined experience of more than 20 years in the industry. Each member of our team has previously held positions in various segments and business units within the local ICT scene, including Principals, Software Development Houses, and multi-million RM turnover local System Integrator companies. Our "HANDS-ON" approach to our work enables us to adapt to the evolving nature of the business and anticipate future trends.

Core Values

Core Values.

Core Values Human Capital

Our expertise in adapting and adopting new technologies is a KEY ASPECT of ours, and we are passionate about upholding values in all aspects of business. This includes how we serve our customers, interact with our colleagues, and engage with our partners and strategic alliances.

Business Strategy

Our motto of 'less is for more' reflects our commitment to providing the right solutions that offer more value on deliverables while minimizing costs.

We Create

At the core of ICT is always the heart of the system, which we believe is the software that drives it. Without the heart beating, the system cannot continue running. As such, we specialize in creating and developing application software.

Why Us?

We provide the fundamental elements of a successful business: the right technologies, solutions, people, and customer service. By combining these elements, we design what is best for the future of our clients.
